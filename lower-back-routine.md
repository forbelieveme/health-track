## Your 4 Poses (5-10 min daily)

1. **Child's Pose** (1-2 min) - Gently stretches lower back
2. **Knee-to-Chest Stretch** (1-2 min) - Releases lower back tension
3. **Supine Twist** (1-2 min) - Decompresses the spine
4. **Happy Baby** (1 min) - Stretches lower back & hips
